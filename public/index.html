About

How I work

I like to understand why something works, not only that it does. When I meet an unfamiliar piece of technology, such as a WebSocket server, a payment gateway's verification step or an email-authentication record, I keep reading past the quick-start until its behaviour makes sense. That habit has been most useful on ResQNet, where a payment that isn't verified on the server, or two organisations claiming the same rescue case at once, has real consequences.

Right now my specialisation is PHP and Laravel: designing and securing REST APIs, integrating third-party services, working with relational databases, testing, and keeping a live platform healthy through CI/CD and cloud hosting. Laravel is where my experience is deepest today, but I treat it as a way into backend engineering in general (HTTP, data modelling, deployment, debugging production) rather than as the boundary of what I work on.

I'm most interested in reliable backend systems, the integrations that connect them, and practical engineering problems in software that real people depend on.

ResQNet

Animal rescue, adoption, donation and health-management platform

A Laravel backend that serves a React Native (Expo) mobile app for the public and web dashboards for rescue organisations and administrators. People report injured animals, apply to adopt and donate; organisations claim rescue cases, dispatch their teams and keep health records for the animals in their care.

  • Versioned REST API (api/v1) for the mobile app, with Sanctum token authentication
  • Role-based access for public users, organisations and super admins using Spatie Permission and policies
  • Real-time updates for rescue cases and adoptions over Laravel Reverb WebSockets
  • Integrations: Khalti donations verified on the server, Firebase Cloud Messaging, transactional email
  • CircleCI runs the test suite against MySQL, then deploys to Heroku from main

My role

Backend lead in a three-member team: API design, authentication, integrations, database, CI/CD, deployment and ongoing maintenance.
